================================================================================
SUMMARY OF FINDINGS
================================================================================

TASK 1: ACTIVATION FUNCTION ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sigmoid Activation:
  • Final Accuracy: 11.70%
  • Problem: Vanishing gradient - gradients become extremely small
  • Loss stayed around 2.3, model couldn't learn

Tanh Activation:
  • Final Accuracy: 96.50%
  • Better than sigmoid because outputs are centered around 0
  • Still suffers from vanishing gradient but less severe

ReLU Activation:
  • Final Accuracy: 96.95%
  • Best performance - no vanishing gradient for positive values
  • Simple computation: max(0, x)
  • Fastest convergence

TASK 2: OPTIMIZER COMPARISON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SGD (Basic):
  • Final Accuracy: 90.45%
  • Slowest convergence
  • Loss curve shows unstable/bumpy descent

SGD with Momentum:
  • Final Accuracy: 96.45%
  • Much smoother than basic SGD
  • Momentum helps overcome local bumps in loss landscape

Adam Optimizer:
  • Final Accuracy: 97.15%
  • Fastest convergence - reached high accuracy by epoch 3
  • Adaptive learning rates for each parameter
  • Best overall performance

TASK 3: REGULARIZATION EFFECTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Without BN, Without Dropout:
  • Accuracy: 94.30%
  • Baseline performance

Without BN, Dropout=0.1:
  • Accuracy: 94.45%
  • Slight improvement from regularization

With BN, Dropout=0.25:
  • Accuracy: 94.35%
  • BatchNorm normalizes layer inputs -> stable training
  • Dropout prevents overfitting by randomly dropping neurons

REQUIRED MODELS PERFORMANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CNN-1 (128 units, Adam, 10 epochs):      97.00%
MLP-1 (512-256-128, SGD, 20 epochs):     88.90%
MLP-2 (256 units, Adam, 15 epochs):      94.45%

KEY TAKEAWAYS
================================================================================
1. ReLU is the best activation for deep networks (avoids vanishing gradient)
2. Adam optimizer converges much faster than SGD variants
3. Batch Normalization + Dropout help prevent overfitting
4. CNN architectures generally perform better than MLP for image data
5. More layers (MLP-1) don't always mean better performance with SGD
